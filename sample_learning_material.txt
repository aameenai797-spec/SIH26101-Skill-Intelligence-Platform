Official statistics are numerical information about the economic, demographic, social and environmental conditions of a country.
Survey design defines the objectives, target population, data collection method and operational plan of a statistical survey.
Sampling is the process of selecting a representative subset from a population to estimate population characteristics.
Data quality includes accuracy, relevance, timeliness, accessibility, coherence and interpretability.
Python is a programming language widely used for data processing, statistical analysis and automation.
SQL is used to store, retrieve and manage structured information in relational databases.
Data privacy refers to the responsible collection, processing, storage and sharing of personal information.
Cybersecurity protects systems, networks and data from unauthorized access, disruption and misuse.
